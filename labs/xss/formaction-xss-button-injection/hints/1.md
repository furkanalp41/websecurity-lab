First, learn what the sanitiser actually blocks. Post a draft with a `<script>`
and an `<img src=x onerror=…>` and read what comes back from the app — they are
gone. This is a real allow-list sanitiser (nh3): scripted payloads do not survive,
so stop trying to run JavaScript.

Now post a draft with a plain `<button>Hi</button>` — it stays. The feature is a
styled call-to-action button. The question is _which attributes_ the sanitiser
lets that button keep.

Look at where your draft is rendered. The editor reviews it on `/admin/review`
(403 for you), and the draft is placed **inside** a `<form method="post">` that
has **no `action`** and carries a hidden anti-CSRF token. A form with no action
submits to the current URL. What could a button do to change that?
