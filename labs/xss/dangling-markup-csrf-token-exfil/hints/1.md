The staff review page reflects your `?u=` value **raw** into an **unquoted** HTML
attribute, and its CSP is `script-src 'none'` — so `<script>` and `onerror` do
nothing. Stop trying to run JavaScript.

But look at what the CSP still allows: `img-src *`. Loading an image is not
"executing a script." And your input goes **raw** into an attribute, so you can
introduce new tags. What could an `<img>` do — just by being _loaded_, with no JS —
that sends data somewhere you control? And what if you opened its `src` and never
closed it?
