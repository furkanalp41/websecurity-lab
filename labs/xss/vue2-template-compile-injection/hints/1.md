Post a widget with a Vue expression, e.g. `<span>hi {{ 7*6 }}</span>`, then note
that the preview is on an admin-only dashboard (`/admin/dashboard` is 403 for
you — only the admin bot loads it). The dashboard runs `Vue.compile()` on your
template at runtime, so your `{{ }}` expressions are **evaluated**, not shown as
text. That is client-side template injection: you control the template, not just
the data.

Your expression runs in the admin's browser. What can you reach from a Vue
expression that gets you to arbitrary JavaScript?
