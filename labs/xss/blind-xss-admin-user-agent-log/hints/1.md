There is no search box or comment form here — so where is the input? OpsConsole
keeps a **visitor access log**, and access logs record the `User-Agent` header of
every request. That header is entirely attacker-controlled, and almost nothing
validates it.

Send a `GET /` with a `User-Agent` of your choosing. Notice the response is the
same status page no matter what you put there — your header is **never reflected
back**. That is what makes this XSS _blind_: you cannot confirm the injection by
viewing your own response.

Staff review the log at an admin-only page (`GET /admin/logs`, 403 for you). If
your header is stored and later rendered there as raw HTML, your code runs in the
**admin's** browser, not yours. How do you even prove a payload fired on a page
you can't load?
