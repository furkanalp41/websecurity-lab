Look at what the forum's BBCode renderer does with `[img]URL[/img]`. It turns it
into `<img src="URL">`. The rest of your post body is HTML-escaped (so `<b>`
comes out as literal `&lt;b&gt;` text — not a tag), but the URL you put between
`[img]` and `[/img]` is dropped **raw** into the `src` attribute.

So markup in the body itself is a dead end. Your one and only injection point is
the `[img]` URL — and it lands **inside** a double-quoted attribute value:

```html
<img src="  your URL here  " />
```

Running a script in _your own_ browser gets you nothing, though. Whose browser
holds the cookie worth stealing, and what page do they load that shows your
post?
