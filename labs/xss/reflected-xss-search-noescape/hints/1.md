Look at what `/?q=` does to your input. Request `/?q=<b>test</b>` and view the
page source. Your `<b>` tags are reflected **without** being turned into
`&lt;b&gt;` — the search heading is rendered with HTML escaping turned off. That
means any markup you put in `q`, including a `<script>`, becomes live HTML.

But running a script in _your own_ browser gets you nothing. Whose browser holds
the cookie worth stealing? What feature on this site lets you hand that person a
URL?
