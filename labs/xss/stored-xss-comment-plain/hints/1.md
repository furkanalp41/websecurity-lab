Post a comment, then notice it does not appear on the front page — it is held
for moderation. Staff review pending comments at an admin-only page, and that
page renders comment bodies as **raw HTML** (Django's `|safe` filter was used,
disabling the automatic escaping).

This is stored XSS: you post the payload, and it runs later in the **admin's**
browser when they moderate. You can't load the moderation page yourself (it's
admin-only, 403) — but you don't need to.
