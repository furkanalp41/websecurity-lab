Stop hammering the registration form. The signup INSERT goes through the ORM, so
quotes you type there are stored as literal characters and never break a query —
you can confirm this by registering `a'b` and seeing it just work.

The bug is not where your data _enters_ the app; it is where your data is _used
again later_. Look at the pages that are built out of your account. Which one is
clearly assembled from a query that depends on **your username**? Ask yourself:
did the app ever check that your username is safe to drop into SQL? It did not —
it only stored it safely. "Stored safely" and "safe to reuse" are not the same
thing.
