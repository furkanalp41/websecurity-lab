Try `region=' -- ` and `region=x' || 'y` and `region=APAC`. All three come back
with either `{"ok": true, "message": "found N reports"}` or a bland
`{"ok": false, "error": "server error"}` — no rows, no SQL text, no useful
timing (the response is jittered on purpose).

You have a real SQL injection: `'` breaks the query, `||` concatenates as
expected. What you don't have is a **way to read data through the response**.
Reframe the goal: what kinds of injections _don't_ need to read data through
the response?

Look at what packages an Oracle application role commonly holds. There's one
in particular that lets a query cause a **side effect** the outside world can
observe — even when the response body is a black box.
