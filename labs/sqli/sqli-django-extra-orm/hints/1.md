"We use the ORM" is doing a lot of load-bearing work in this team's confidence.
The ORM parameterises the queries **it** builds for you — `.filter()`,
`.get()`, and friends. But Django also ships escape hatches that take **raw SQL**
strings: `QuerySet.extra()`, `Manager.raw()`, and `RawSQL()`. Inside those, you
are back to hand-writing SQL, and nothing is bound for you.

Confirm the sink is real before you craft anything: send `/search?q='` (a single
quote). A parameterised query would just return no matches. Instead you get a
loud database **syntax error** — proof your input is being treated as SQL, not as
data. The app is deliberately chatty about these errors; read them.
