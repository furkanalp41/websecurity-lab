You have probably been changing things in the URL and the page has ignored you.
Step back and look at what the server hands you on your very first visit.

Reload the page with devtools (or a proxy) open and watch the response headers.
The site issues a `Set-Cookie: TrackingId=...` and then greets you with a banner
that clearly depends on that value — it even prints your tracking id back to you.

So your input _is_ reaching server-side logic. It is just arriving somewhere
testers habitually treat as read-only and trustworthy, because "the server set
it." Ask yourself: is a cookie really out of your control? What happens if you
replay the request and send a `TrackingId` of your own choosing?
